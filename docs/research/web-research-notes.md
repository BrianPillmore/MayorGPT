# Web Research Notes

## Municipal professional sources
- ICMA: council-manager form and role separation.
- GFOA: multi-step, collaborative budget process and financial policy best practices.
- IIMC: clerk responsibilities around minutes, records, contracts, elections, and public interface.
- FEMA: preparedness/response/recovery/mitigation framing for emergency management.
- APWA: public works as a combination of assets, policies, personnel, municipal services, and infrastructure stewardship.

## Working implication for this repo
Municipal AI should mirror real city roles and workflows rather than forcing everything into one generic
“assistant” prompt.
