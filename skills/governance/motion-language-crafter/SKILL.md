# Motion Language Crafter

**Purpose:** Convert intent ("I want this approved, but with conditions") into precise, votable motions, with alternates, so the record is clean and the action is legally effective. See `motion-template.md`.

## Required inputs
- Agenda item, staff recommendation, and the instrument being acted on (ordinance, resolution, contract, permit)
- Desired outcome and any conditions
- Vote threshold and council rules of procedure (Robert's Rules or an adopted variant; local rules control)

## Workflow
1. **Name the vehicle:** adopt, introduce/first reading, approve, authorize, deny, continue to a date certain, refer, table.
2. **State the object exactly:** by number and title, with attachments by name ("Exhibit A dated ...").
3. **Add operative content:** authorize the manager/mayor to execute; not-to-exceed amounts; funding source or budget amendment; conditions; effective date.
4. **Quasi-judicial or land-use items:** the motion must adopt specific findings or direct staff to prepare them; "I move to deny" without findings invites reversal (see `land-use-findings-builder`).
5. **Draft the family of motions:** main motion, a friendly amendment path, substitute motion, motion to continue, motion to deny (with reasons), and a motion to reconsider (who may move it and by when, per your rules).
6. **Check mechanics:** requires a second? Debatable? Amendable? Vote needed? Does a tie fail? How are abstentions or recusals counted (varies; verify)? Roll-call required for ordinances or expenditures?
7. **Read-back:** the clerk should be able to restate the motion verbatim; keep it to one or two sentences plus attachments.

## Output structure
- Primary motion (verbatim)
- Alternates: amend, continue, deny, refer
- Required findings or conditions list
- Voting requirements and roll-call note
- Signature/attestation blocks needed (mayor, clerk, attorney as to form)

## Example
> "I move that the council adopt Resolution 2026-__, approving the agreement with __ in an amount not to exceed $__ from Fund __, and authorizing the City Manager to execute it substantially in the form attached as Exhibit A."

## Red flags / escalate to the city attorney
- Motion would amend an ordinance on first reading in a way that restarts readings
- Conditions beyond the staff report with no findings support
- A member who should recuse is the maker or seconder

## Common mistakes
- Vague motions ("approve as discussed")
- Motions to table used to kill an item when the rules treat tabling differently
- Omitting the dollar cap and funding source

*Not legal advice; local rules control.*
