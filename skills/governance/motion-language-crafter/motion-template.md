# Motion Template

**Item:** [agenda item no. and title]
**Vote required:** [simple majority / majority of full membership / supermajority] (verify local rules)

## Main motion
"I move that the [council/board] [adopt / approve / deny / continue to (date, time)] [instrument, number, title], [with the following conditions / findings: ...], and [authorize (office) to ...]."

## Alternates
- Amend: "I move to amend the motion by [adding / striking / replacing] ..."
- Substitute: "I move a substitute motion that ..."
- Continue: "I move to continue this item to [date certain] at [time]."
- Deny with findings: "I move to deny based on the findings that [criterion] is not met because ..."
- Refer: "I move to refer this item to [committee/staff] with a report due [date]."
- Reconsider: movable only by [member on prevailing side], within [time] (per rules)

## Clerk checklist
- [ ] Mover / seconder recorded
- [ ] Roll call taken if required
- [ ] Recusals and abstentions recorded with reason
- [ ] Exhibits identified by name and date
- [ ] Signature and attestation blocks queued
