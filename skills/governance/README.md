# Governance

Skills for agenda management, legislative drafting, meetings, appointments, and governance process.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `agenda-packet-builder` | Build a city council or board agenda packet with readiness checks, missing attachments, hearing/publication flags, and item-by-item support notes. Templates: `checklist.md`, `packet-template.md`. |
| `board-appointment-check` | Screen board and commission appointments for eligibility, term logic, ward/residency rules, and attendance concerns. |
| `charter-code-crosscheck` | Check whether a draft action fits the city's charter, code, local rules, and meeting process before it moves forward. |
| `consent-calendar-screen` | Screen proposed consent-calendar items for eligibility, hidden controversy, fiscal thresholds, and legal-notice problems before the agenda is published. |
| `council-meeting-brief` | Prepare the mayor, council, manager, or clerk for a meeting with question lists, motions, and item-by-item watch notes. |
| `election-calendar-planner` | Map key dates and task owners for municipal filing periods, elections, onboarding, and related notices or briefings. |
| `initiative-referendum-recall-guide` | Create a neutral process guide for initiative, referendum, or recall questions using local charter and state-law references. |
| `legislative-history-brief` | Build a concise history of an ordinance, resolution, policy, or recurring issue from prior agendas, minutes, and staff memos. |
| `motion-language-crafter` | Turn a desired decision into clean motion language and identify amendments, findings, and signature blocks that may be needed. Templates: `motion-template.md`. |
| `open-meeting-risk-check` | Review proposed meeting activity, messaging, workshops, emails, or serial conversations for meeting-law and transparency risk. |
| `ordinance-drafter` | Draft or revise a municipal ordinance with issue spotting around authority, amendment targets, enforcement, effective dates, and procedural steps. Templates: `ordinance-template.md`. |
| `parliamentary-procedure-coach` | Coach a chair or member through the correct parliamentary mechanics for a live or anticipated procedural situation, using the body's adopted rules first. |
| `public-hearing-prep` | Prepare hearing scripts, findings, notice checkpoints, public-comment capture structure, and closing action options. |
| `public-records-response` | Triage a public records or FOIA request into responsive records, applicable exemptions, required notifications, and a legally sufficient draft response letter. Templates: `response-letter-template.md`. |
| `resolution-drafter` | Draft a municipal resolution for appointments, approvals, findings, recognitions, or formal policy statements. Templates: `resolution-template.md`. |
| `staff-report-builder` | Create a decision-ready staff report with summary, background, analysis, options, recommendation, fiscal impact, and attachment list. Templates: `staff-report-template.md`. |

**Notes:** These skills provide issue-spotting and drafting frameworks, not legal advice. State and local law varies; always verify deadlines, thresholds, and procedures against your own charter, code, and state statutes, and route flagged items to the city attorney.
