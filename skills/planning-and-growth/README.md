# Planning And Growth

Skills for zoning, subdivision, annexation, development review, fiscal analysis of growth, incentives, housing, downtown, and development agreements. Skills assume land-use law varies by state and flag where counsel must confirm authority, standards, and process.

| Skill | Use it to |
|---|---|
| annexation-brief | Assess annexation pathway, service plan, and 10 to 20 year fiscal effect |
| comp-plan-crosswalk | Test a proposal against adopted plan policies with citations |
| development-agreement-review | Review obligations, triggers, public cost, and enforceability (template included) |
| downtown-project-concept | Shape a downtown concept around land productivity, phasing, and partners |
| economic-development-incentive-memo | Summarize incentive cost, but-for evidence, and controls (template included) |
| economic-gardening-analysis | Compare local-business support against recruitment incentives |
| exaction-nexus-review | Test conditions and fees for nexus and rough proportionality (template included) |
| fiscal-impact-analysis | Compute net fiscal contribution, payback, and value per acre (template included) |
| housing-policy-options | Build housing options tied to diagnosed needs and city authority |
| infrastructure-liability-assessment | Quantify the hidden annual infrastructure funding gap |
| street-design-fiscal-analysis | Add a fiscal-productivity view to corridor decisions alongside safety and engineering |
| subdivision-case-brief | Prepare a plat decision brief with conditions and shot-clock notes |
| tif-district-analysis | Analyze TIF eligibility, increments, overlapping-jurisdiction effects, and exit |
| zoning-case-analysis | Analyze rezonings and permits against the correct legal standard (template included) |
