# Contract Issue Spotter

**Purpose:** First-pass issue spotting on a city contract before the attorney's formal review. It does not replace legal review or approve terms.

## Required inputs
- Draft contract with all exhibits, scope of work, pricing, insurance requirements
- Procurement file: method (competitive bid, RFP, cooperative purchase, sole source), approvals, thresholds
- Funding source (general fund, grant, bond); sponsoring department

## Workflow
1. **Authority and procurement:** who may sign (dollar limits, council approval), whether competitive procurement rules were followed (verify thresholds), and whether grant or federal funds attach required clauses.
2. **Parties and scope:** exact legal names; scope specific enough to measure performance; deliverables, acceptance criteria, schedule; subcontractor limits.
3. **Term and renewal:** multi-year obligations often need annual appropriation language ("subject to appropriation") so the city is not bound beyond funded years; auto-renewals and evergreen clauses are a red flag.
4. **Payment:** not-to-exceed cap, milestone or hourly basis, retainage, prompt-payment rules, change-order authority and limits, escalators.
5. **Risk allocation:** indemnity (is it one-sided or limited by state law and governmental immunity?), limitation of liability, insurance types and limits, additional-insured and waiver of subrogation, performance and payment bonds on public works.
6. **Public-entity provisions:** public records compliance and vendor duty to cooperate, retention, confidentiality clauses that conflict with disclosure law, open-meetings, non-discrimination, prevailing wage, local-preference rules, e-verify or similar state mandates (verify).
7. **Termination and remedies:** termination for convenience, cure periods, transition assistance, data return, dispute resolution, venue, governing law (typically the city's state).
8. **Data and technology:** ownership of data, security, breach notice, auto-renewing software terms, click-through terms.
9. **Conflicts:** vendor relationships with officials or staff (see `ethics-conflict-check`).

## Output structure
- Issue table: clause | concern | severity (High/Med/Low) | suggested fix
- Missing exhibits and approvals
- Questions for the vendor and department
- Short list for the attorney

## Red flags / escalate to the city attorney
- Indemnity or liability clauses by the city; arbitration or out-of-state venue
- Multi-year commitment without appropriation language
- Confidentiality terms that limit public-records disclosure
- Sole-source or emergency procurement; related-party vendor

## Common mistakes
- Reviewing the body but not the exhibits and vendor terms of service
- Missing automatic renewal dates
- Treating insurance certificates as optional

*Not legal advice.*
