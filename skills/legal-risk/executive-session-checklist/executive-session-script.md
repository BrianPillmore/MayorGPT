# Executive Session Script (adapt to state law)

**Before the meeting:** attorney confirms ground; agenda item cites ground per statute: [citation to be verified].

**Chair, in open session:**
"The council will now recess to executive session under [statute/code section], to discuss [description required by law, e.g., pending litigation: case caption / real property: parcel and negotiating party / labor: negotiator]. We expect the session to last about [__] minutes. [A vote is required: motion, second, vote.] We will reconvene in open session [here / at ____]."

**At start of closed session:** attorney confirms ground, attendance list, and that discussion will stay within the ground. Note start time.

**During:** no final votes or actions (unless statute allows); keep notes per statute; no recordings except as the statute requires.

**At end:** attorney confirms the discussion remained within the ground. Note end time.

**Chair, back in open session:**
"The council has returned from executive session. [Report-out required by law: e.g., 'Direction was given to the City Attorney.'] [Any vote taken now, in public: motion, second, roll call.]"

**Clerk:** log start/end time, attendees, ground cited, certification; store the confidential record under seal per retention rules.
