# Legal Risk

Skills for legal issue spotting, records, ethics, contracts, and litigation-related process.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `contract-issue-spotter` | Review a draft contract for scope, term, payment, indemnity, insurance, records, termination, and operational red flags. |
| `ethics-conflict-check` | Spot ethics, disclosure, gift, recusal, personal-interest, or appearance issues in a proposed city action. |
| `executive-session-checklist` | Verify that a proposed closed session has proper statutory grounds, compliant notice, and the documentation needed to defend the session from a sunshine-law challenge. Templates: `executive-session-script.md`. |
| `first-amendment-comment-limits` | Evaluate proposed public-comment rules, speaker removals, and social-media moderation against First Amendment limits for government-run forums. |
| `franchise-utility-review` | Review a franchise or utility-related action for authority, rate/process implications, and approval steps. |
| `labor-employment-issue-scan` | Screen a personnel or workplace issue for policy, due-process, leave, retaliation, wage-hour, or discrimination risk. |
| `land-use-findings-builder` | Draft or test zoning and land-use findings so they track adopted criteria rather than preference alone. Templates: `findings-template.md`. |
| `litigation-hold-checklist` | Create a defensible litigation-hold plan with custodians, systems, retention steps, and communication notes. Templates: `litigation-hold-notice-template.md`. |
| `ordinance-enforceability-check` | Review a draft ordinance for vague language, missing definitions, due-process issues, and enforcement practicality. |
| `records-request-triage` | Triage a public records request into likely custodians, date ranges, exclusions, sensitive material, and response steps. |
| `records-retention-trigger-check` | Determine retention periods, event triggers, and destruction holds for a category of city records before disposal or response. |

**Notes:** These skills provide issue-spotting and drafting frameworks, not legal advice. State and local law varies; always verify deadlines, thresholds, and procedures against your own charter, code, and state statutes, and route flagged items to the city attorney.
