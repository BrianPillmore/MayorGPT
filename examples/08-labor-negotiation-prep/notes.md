# Notes: Labor Negotiation Prep

## What made this output strong
1. It stays at the position level; no individual employee data.
2. It converts legal uncertainty into specific questions for counsel.
3. It refuses to invent cost figures and instructs Finance how to compute them.
4. Communications guidance is short and flagged for counsel review.

## Common mistakes
- Treating the scope of bargaining as national law; it is state-specific.
- Using a public AI account for confidential strategy.
- Letting AI propose a "bottom line" position.
- Sharing the draft broadly with managers who do not need it.

## Variations
- For a reduction-in-force or reorganization, use `prompts/people-and-hr/workforce-reorganization.md` with counsel.
- For comparable-city benchmarking, attach a cited comparables table and use `skills/data-and-research/policy-research-synthesizer/SKILL.md`.
