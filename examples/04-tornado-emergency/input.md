## Field Reports as of 9:30 PM (compilied from dispatch, department calls, and first responder radio)

### Damage Summary — Residential

**Thornwood Estates (NE quadrant):**
- Approximately 40–50 homes with visible roof damage (aerial observation by Fire Department drone, 8:45 PM)
- 6 structures with apparent wall/structural collapse (flagged by Ladder 3 crew, confirmed by structural assessment team lead)
- 200+ residents in affected blocks
- **Status of structural assessment:** In progress. Structural engineer contracted with city on scene. Preliminary tags being issued. As of 9:30 PM: 6 RED (do not enter), 14 YELLOW (limited entry), remainder not yet assessed.

**Millvale Heights (adjacent subdivision):**
- Approximately 15–20 homes with roof damage (reported by PW crew, not yet verified by structural team)
- No collapse structures reported
- **Status:** Not yet assessed by structural team

### Damage Summary — Public Infrastructure

- 7 streets with downed power lines (reported by Police, confirmed by utility company notification)
- Power company (Midcoast Electric) reports approximately 2,400 customers without power in affected area; restoration estimate: 24–72 hours
- Northgate Elementary School: roof damage to gymnasium wing reported by principal (telephone report, 8:10 PM); building was unoccupied at time of tornado
- City Public Works yard on Commerce Street: one storage building with roof damage; city equipment reported operational

### Injuries / Casualties

- 3 injuries confirmed by Millvale FD transport to Regional Medical Center (Millvale FD incident report, 8:30 PM)
- 1 additional injury reported by Police at 2211 Briarwood Court — status unconfirmed; no transport record yet
- No confirmed fatalities as of 9:30 PM
- **NOTE: Search of all RED-tagged structures has not been completed. Potential for additional casualties not ruled out.**

### Shelter Operations

- Millvale Community Center (840 Oak Avenue): Red Cross emergency shelter opened 8:45 PM
- Current shelter occupancy: 37 individuals
- Red Cross shelter capacity: 200
- Additional shelter at First Baptist Fellowship (1200 River Road): available if needed; not yet activated

### Resource Status

- Millvale FD: All units deployed. Mutual aid request submitted to neighboring Crestview FD (response: 2 engines en route, ETA 10:15 PM)
- Millvale PD: 14 of 16 patrol units on scene; 2 units on routine call response
- Public Works: 4 crews deployed for road clearance and traffic control; additional crew on call
- Structural assessment team: 2 engineers on site, 1 more en route
- CERT (Community Emergency Response Team): 12 volunteers activated, deployed to shelter

### State/Regional Contacts

- State Emergency Management Division notified 8:00 PM; duty officer acknowledges, advises to document all costs and maintain incident logs per FEMA reimbursement requirements
- County Emergency Management: notified and monitoring; no county assets requested yet
- FEMA pre-contact: Not yet made. State advised this is appropriate once damage assessment is further along.
