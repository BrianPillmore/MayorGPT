## Finance Director's Preliminary Notes

- The $1,240,600 revenue shortfall is driven primarily by sales tax ($704K projected gap) and franchise fees ($371K projected gap).
- The franchise fee gap may partially correct — one cable franchise payment expected March 15 was received late; approximately $180,000 of the franchise gap may be timing, not structural.
- The $96,200 expenditure savings offset the revenue shortfall partially, leaving a net projected gap of approximately $1,144,400.
- Adding the police overtime overrun of $528,000 (which is not yet fully reflected in the annualized projection because two of the investigations are expected to conclude by May), the total exposure is approximately **$1,400,000–$1,600,000** depending on how quickly the investigations close and whether the sworn vacancies are filled.

---

## Policy Constraints

- **General Fund Reserve Policy:** Larkspur Creek's fund balance policy requires maintaining a minimum reserve of 15% of annual expenditures ($3.69M at current budget). Current unassigned reserve is $4.1M — approximately $410,000 above the policy floor.
- **Charter Section 7.3:** Budget amendments exceeding 5% of any department's adopted appropriation require council approval by resolution. The police overrun is currently at 8.1% — it triggers the charter threshold.
- **Mid-year amendment deadline:** The city's budget ordinance requires that any mid-year amendment be adopted by April 30.
