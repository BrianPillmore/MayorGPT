## Application Summary (from applicant submittal)

**Case Number:** RZ-2026-0031  
**Applicant:** Cortland Development Group, LLC  
**Property Owner:** Meridian Land Partners, LLC  
**Site Address:** 4401 Meridian Bluff Road, Halcyon Ridge  
**Parcel ID:** 14-0201-004-0000  
**Acreage:** 14.2 acres  
**Current Zoning:** R-1 (Single-Family Residential, 4 du/ac max)  
**Requested Zoning:** RM-2 (Medium-Density Residential, 18 du/ac max)  
**Proposed Use:** 210-unit market-rate apartment community (mix of 1BR and 2BR)

**Applicant narrative excerpt:**
> "The subject property is located adjacent to the Meridian Bluff Commercial Corridor, a Regional Activity Center designated in the 2022 Comprehensive Plan. The Plan's Housing Element calls for increasing the supply of multi-family residential within one-quarter mile of Regional Activity Centers. The project will provide 210 units of market-rate housing to address the city's documented housing shortage, with landscaped buffers and a 30-foot setback from the adjacent R-1 neighborhood to the north and east."

## Comprehensive Plan Excerpts (2022 Halcyon Ridge Comprehensive Plan)

**Housing Element, Policy H-4:**
> "Encourage medium and high-density residential development within one-quarter mile of designated Regional Activity Centers, subject to compatibility requirements."

**Land Use Element, Policy LU-7:**
> "Transitions from higher-intensity to lower-intensity land uses should be achieved through graduated density buffers or the use of physical features such as roads, drainage corridors, or topographic changes."

**Figure LU-3 (Future Land Use Map):** The subject parcel is shown as "Suburban Residential" on the Future Land Use Map, not "Medium-Density Residential." The Regional Activity Center boundary runs along the south edge of the parcel. The northern two-thirds of the parcel is mapped Suburban Residential.

## Zoning Code Excerpts (Halcyon Ridge Municipal Code, Article 4)

**Section 4.12.030 — RM-2 Criteria for Approval:**

A rezoning to RM-2 may be approved when the governing body finds that the request:

(a) is consistent with the adopted Comprehensive Plan;  
(b) is compatible with surrounding land uses through design, density transition, or buffering;  
(c) does not create infrastructure demand that exceeds available capacity in the water, sewer, and transportation systems serving the site;  
(d) does not constitute spot zoning inconsistent with the pattern of zoning and development in the area.

## Public Works Utility Capacity Memo

**From:** Director of Public Works, Halcyon Ridge  
**To:** Planning Director  
**Date:** March 12, 2026  
**Re:** RZ-2026-0031 Infrastructure Assessment (Preliminary)

Preliminary review indicates:
- **Water:** The 12-inch main on Meridian Bluff Road has adequate capacity. A hydraulic model confirming fire flow at the RM-2 density is **pending** (expected by April 3).
- **Sewer:** The 8-inch gravity sewer serving this area is at 74% capacity based on current flows. At 210 units (assuming 2.1 persons/unit, 65 gpd/person), the estimated additional daily load is approximately 28,665 gpd. Peak factor analysis **not yet completed.**
- **Traffic:** A Traffic Impact Analysis (TIA) is required per city policy for developments generating more than 100 peak-hour trips. The applicant's TIA was submitted March 3; Public Works review is **in progress.**

No capacity confirmation letter has been issued. Final determination pending completion of model runs.

## Neighborhood Petition (received March 18, 2026)

**From:** Meridian Bluff Homeowners Association, on behalf of 147 signatory residents  
**To:** Halcyon Ridge Planning Commission

Key objections summarized:
1. Density incompatibility — surrounding R-1 lots average 0.25 acres; 18 du/ac is a 4.5x increase.
2. Traffic — Meridian Bluff Road already experiences peak-hour congestion near the school zone at Harrington Elementary, located 0.4 miles from the site.
3. Stormwater — the parcel includes a low-lying area that historically floods; neighbors report the drainage easement along the east property line has not been maintained.
4. Future Land Use Map designation — the parcel is shown as Suburban Residential, not Medium-Density Residential.
5. Spot zoning — no other RM-2 parcels are located within 1,200 feet of the site on three sides.
