## Relevant Provisions (from State Open Meetings Act, provided to city attorney)

**[State] Open Meetings Act, Section 551.001 — Definitions:**
"'Meeting' means a deliberation between a quorum of the governmental body, or between a quorum of members of the governmental body and another person, at which public business or public policy over which the governmental body has supervision or control is discussed or considered, or at which the governmental body takes formal action. The term does not include the gathering of a quorum of a governmental body at a social function unrelated to public business of the body."

**Section 551.002 — Open Meeting Required:**
"Every regular, special, or called meeting of a governmental body shall be open to the public."

**Section 551.143 — Criminal Penalty (conspiracy to circumvent):**
"A member or group of members of a governmental body commits an offense if the member or group of members knowingly conspires to circumvent this chapter by meeting in numbers less than a quorum for the purpose of secret deliberations."

**Section 551.101 — Notice Required:**
"A governmental body shall give written notice of the date, hour, place, and subject of each meeting held by the governmental body."

## City Charter Provision (Denton Bluffs City Charter, Section 5.8)

"No member of the City Council shall communicate with other members of the Council in a manner intended to deliberate on any pending matter or to commit votes prior to a duly noticed public meeting. The City Council shall adopt and maintain a communications policy consistent with state open meetings law."

**Note:** The city does not currently have a written council communications policy. The City Clerk's office confirmed this on March 21.
