## Part A: First public notice (6:45 AM)

**WATER SERVICE DISRUPTION: Linden Street area (Update 1)**

A water main broke on Linden Street near 4th Avenue early this morning. About 1,800 homes and businesses between 2nd Avenue, 9th Avenue, Oak Street, and Mill Road have low or no water pressure.

**What we know**
- Crews arrived at 5:50 AM and have shut off the broken section.
- Repairs are expected to take 8 to 10 hours. This is an estimate and may change once crews see the pipe.

**What we do not know yet**
- The cause of the break.
- Whether the water needs to be boiled before drinking. We have asked the state drinking water regulator for guidance and will tell you as soon as we hear.

**What you can do now**
- Do not use hot water or run the dishwasher or washing machine until pressure returns; you may draw in sediment.
- If you have water for drinking, keep it. A water pickup point is planned for the Community Center, 10 AM. We will confirm by 9 AM.
- If you or a neighbor depends on water for medical care, call 555-0100 and press 2.

**Next update: 9:00 AM** on the city website, text alerts, and social media. Spanish and large-print versions: [link]. For TTY/relay, call 555-0101.
