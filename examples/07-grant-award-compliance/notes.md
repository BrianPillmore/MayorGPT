# Notes: Grant Award and Compliance

## What made this output strong
1. Obligations are tied to clauses, owners, and dates.
2. It flags the unappropriated match and the pre-effective-date cost trap first.
3. It lists questions for the funder instead of guessing.

## Common mistakes
- Treating the application narrative as the compliance document; the agreement controls.
- Letting a department start procurement before approval and appropriation.
- Ignoring cash-flow requirements of reimbursement grants.
- Missing special conditions that are in attachments.

## Variations
- For a direct federal award, attach the full terms and conditions and have Finance confirm which cost and procurement rules apply.
- For a subrecipient arrangement where the city passes funds on, add subrecipient monitoring steps.
- Use `prompts/monthly/grant-pipeline-review.md` monthly to report status.
