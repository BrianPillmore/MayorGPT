Dear Ms. Okafor,

Thank you for writing, and I am sorry it came to this.

An eleven-day repair — with inadequate communication throughout — is not what residents of Weston Falls should experience, and it is not what this city's infrastructure crews are capable of at their best. I have read your letter carefully, and I want to respond to you personally rather than send another form communication.

Here is what I know happened. When the water main broke on February 6th, crews responded quickly and isolated the break that morning. What they found was an older cast iron pipe requiring specialized parts that were not in our local supply inventory. Sourcing those parts took longer than it should have. A crew staffing issue during the week of February 10th added further delay. The repair was completed on February 17th.

That explanation is not an excuse. The road hazard should have been marked immediately and continuously. You should have received a timeline and proactive status updates — not silence followed by a form letter. The Utilities Department's March 2nd response was not acceptable, and I take responsibility for the city's failure to communicate with you the way you deserved.

Here is what I am asking to happen next. I have directed the City Manager to review this case as part of a broader look at our infrastructure repair communication protocols. You will hear from the City Manager's office within ten business days with a specific account of the process review and any changes that result. If you have additional concerns in the meantime, you may contact my office directly at 940-555-1100 or mayorsofficewf@westonftx.gov.

You have been a part of this community for 22 years. That matters. Your frustration was earned, and your decision to speak publicly was your right. I hope this response is a better one.

Sincerely,
