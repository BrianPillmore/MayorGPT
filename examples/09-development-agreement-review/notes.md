# Notes: Development Agreement Review

## What made this output strong
1. It prioritizes provisions rather than listing everything equally.
2. It identifies absent terms, not just problematic ones.
3. It routes each legal point to Land Use Counsel as a question.
4. It does not assert state-law limits it cannot know.

## Common mistakes
- Treating a developer's term sheet as a starting point for the city's position.
- Letting vesting terms pass without an independent fee-rate analysis.
- Forgetting that a rezoning and a development agreement are separate actions with separate processes.
- Overlooking assignment and default mechanics.
