# Example 9: Development Agreement Review

## Scenario

A developer has submitted a draft development agreement for a 40-acre mixed-use project in the fictional City of Westgate (pop. 28,000). The draft requests a long vesting period, a city reimbursement for oversized utility lines, and a fee-waiver package. The Planning Director needs a structured review before negotiations begin, and Land Use Counsel needs a list of legal questions.

All names, parcels, and figures are fictional. Development agreement authority, maximum terms, and vesting rules vary by state.

## Why this combination works

A development agreement binds future councils. Review needs legal, planning, and fiscal lenses, preserved separately (see `stacks/land-use-hearing-stack.md` and `stacks/ORCHESTRATION_PROTOCOL.md`).

## Key cautions

- **State law limits** on development agreements, vesting, and fee waivers vary. Land Use Counsel decides what is permissible.
- **Do not negotiate by AI draft.** The output is an issues list and question set, not a counteroffer.
- **Check the record.** Any findings later used in a hearing must cite the administrative record.
- **Confidentiality.** Developer proprietary financials may be exempt from disclosure depending on state; confirm before pasting into any tool.
