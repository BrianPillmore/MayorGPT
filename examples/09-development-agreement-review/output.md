# Output: Development Agreement Review Memo

> **How to read this file.** Illustrative working draft. Land Use Counsel must review all legal observations. Annotations in blockquotes; delete before use.

## 1. Summary
The term sheet contains several provisions that shift cost or risk to the city and bind future councils. The most significant are the 25-year vesting, the utility reimbursement funded from an under-reserved fund, the free assignment, and the long cure and arbitration path. Recommended posture: request a revised term sheet before substantive negotiation.

## 2. Clause review

| Provision | Observation | Priority | Who confirms |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25-year vesting of regulations, fees, and impact fee rates | Far longer than the city's prior 10-year practice. Freezing fee rates can lock in rates below actual cost over time. | High | Land Use Counsel (state limits on term and vesting) |
| Utility oversizing reimbursement over 10 years from utility revenue | Creates a multi-year obligation from a fund below its reserve target. Need documented cost basis and nexus to city-wide benefit. | High | Finance, Utilities, Counsel |
| 50 percent permit fee waiver on 150 units | Foregone revenue; determine whether a waiver is permissible and whether it must be funded from another source. | Medium | Counsel, Finance |
| City maintains 2-acre park | Ongoing cost not in term sheet; quantify. | Medium | Parks, Finance |
| Streets dedicated after one-year warranty | Warranty period may be shorter than defect emergence. | Medium | Public Works |
| 180-day cure then arbitration | Slows city's ability to enforce. Consider shorter cure and venue provisions. | High | Counsel |
| Free assignment | City loses control over who performs obligations. Require consent or assumption agreement and financial capability showing. | High | Counsel |
| Missing: performance milestones, security for improvements, public benefit commitments | Absent terms mean the city has no leverage on delivery timing. | High | Planning, Counsel |

## 4. Consistency with plans
The comp plan designates medium density; the proposal's density must be checked against the comp plan (`comp-plan-crosswalk`). The rezoning is a separate legislative action and the agreement must not pre-commit Council's discretion on it. Counsel to confirm how state law treats this.

## 5. Questions for Land Use Counsel
1. Maximum term and vesting permitted by state law for development agreements; required procedures (notice, hearings).
2. Whether the city may agree to fee waivers and how existing fees must be treated.
3. Enforceability of reimbursement obligations and their effect on utility bond covenants.
4. Limits on arbitration clauses for the city.
5. Whether the agreement may bind future councils on discretionary approvals.

1. Send the developer a request for revised term sheet addressing the high-priority items.

3. Brief Council in appropriate session (counsel advises on format).
