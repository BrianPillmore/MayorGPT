# Input: Development Agreement Review, City of Westgate (fictional)

## Developer's draft term sheet (summary)
- Site: 40 acres, currently agricultural, within city limits, zoned for low-density residential; rezoning to mixed-use requested separately
- Program: 320 residential units, 60,000 sq ft commercial
- Term: 25 years; vested rights to current regulations, fees, and impact fee rates for the full term
- Utilities: developer builds oversized water and sewer lines; city reimburses the oversizing cost over 10 years from utility revenue
- Fees: 50 percent reduction in permit fees for the first 150 units
- Parks: developer dedicates 2 acres; city accepts maintenance
- Streets: public streets dedicated to city after one-year warranty
- Default and remedies: city may terminate only after 180-day cure period and arbitration
- Assignment: freely assignable to any successor without city consent

## City context
- Adopted comprehensive plan designates the area as "future growth, medium density."
- Utility fund reserve is below policy target.
- Prior city development agreements capped at 10 years.
- Estimated infrastructure condition and cost data attached for nearby streets.
