# Stacks

A stack bundles the agents, prompts, and skills needed for a specific recurring municipal workflow. Pick the stack that matches the situation, follow its sequence, and attach the source material it calls for.

For multi-agent handoff format, output preservation, human review gates, and accountability tracing, see `ORCHESTRATION_PROTOCOL.md` in this directory.

---

## The stacks

| Stack | Use when |
|---|---|
| `mayor-office-stack.md` | The Mayor needs remarks, a constituent response, a press release, or crisis visibility — and must appear as the public face of the city. |
| `council-stack.md` | The agenda is being built, a Council member needs item background, a public hearing requires findings, or a motion or resolution needs to be drafted. |
| `city-manager-stack.md` | Weekly operations need triaging, a Council directive must become a work plan, or department staff reports for the same meeting are uncoordinated. |
| `budget-season-stack.md` | The annual budget calendar opens, mid-year variance numbers land, a CIP list needs prioritization, or an audit finding requires a management response. |
| `land-use-hearing-stack.md` | A significant rezoning, variance, conditional use permit, or development agreement reaches the staff review or Planning Commission stage. |
| `emergency-operations-stack.md` | A declared or imminent emergency requires coordinated city response — or a large-scale planned event requires significant operational coordination. |
| `public-safety-review-stack.md` | Quarterly public safety KPIs are due for Council, a community safety concern needs structured analysis, or a major incident triggers a public accountability review. |
| `labor-and-personnel-stack.md` | A recruitment opens, a disciplinary process requires documentation, a formal workplace investigation is initiated, or performance review cycles begin. |
| `grant-pursuit-stack.md` | A new federal or state program needs eligibility screening, an application deadline is approaching, or a grant compliance deliverable is due. |
| `intergovernmental-relations-stack.md` | A state legislative session opens and bills need tracking, a regional COG or MPO item requires a city position, or an interlocal agreement is being negotiated. |
| `fiscal-productivity-stack.md` | A major development proposal, TIF or incentive request, annexation petition, or new infrastructure project needs a full fiscal reality check before Council approval. |
| `annual-report-and-state-of-the-city-stack.md` | The annual report cycle opens, or the Mayor or City Manager is preparing a State of the City address that must be tied to verified performance and financial data. |
| `new-administration-transition-stack.md` | An election is certified, new Council members or a new Mayor are sworn in, or a new City Manager starts and needs briefings, onboarding, and a campaign-to-governance translation. |
| `utilities-rate-case-stack.md` | A rate study is commissioned, a utility is under-recovering its cost of service, or a rate adjustment is heading to Council with notice and hearing requirements. |
| `downtown-redevelopment-stack.md` | A downtown plan, catalytic site, or developer partnership or incentive request needs concept framing, fiscal and infrastructure analysis, and development agreement review. |

Stack formats are consistent: Best for, When to activate, Combine, Sequence, Watch for. When adding a stack, follow `ORCHESTRATION_PROTOCOL.md` (handoff format, preserved specialist outputs, Gates 1-4, accountability trace) and link only to files that exist.
