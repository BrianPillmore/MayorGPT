# Planning and Land Use Rubric

Use this for zoning case analyses, staff reports, findings, development agreement reviews, comprehensive plan crosswalks, subdivision briefs, annexation briefs, and fiscal impact analyses.

Score each criterion from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong).

## Criteria
1. **Correct decision type**: Does it identify whether the action is legislative, quasi-judicial, or administrative, and what that implies for process, ex parte contact, and standard of review? It must say where state law controls rather than assert a rule.
2. **Standards and criteria applied**: Does it apply the actual code criteria or approval standards that were supplied, instead of generic planning principles?
3. **Record-based findings**: Is each finding tied to specific evidence in the record, with a reference? No finding relies on facts not in the attachments.
4. **Plan consistency**: Does it analyze consistency with the comprehensive plan and adopted area plans, naming specific goals or policies?
5. **Fiscal and infrastructure realism**: Does it consider revenue versus service cost, infrastructure capital cost, and long-term maintenance liability, with assumptions and ranges?
6. **Procedural compliance**: Does it flag notice, hearing, timeline, and appeal requirements to be confirmed by the City Attorney or Land Use Counsel?
7. **Legal risk flags**: Does it identify risks such as vested rights, spot zoning, takings, due process, and conditions-nexus issues as questions for counsel rather than conclusions?
8. **Balanced public input handling**: Does it summarize public comment fairly, by theme and count, and distinguish relevant criteria from non-relevant concerns without dismissing residents?
9. **Neutral, role-appropriate recommendation**: Is the recommendation clearly staff's with its basis stated, and does it avoid pre-judging a quasi-judicial matter?
10. **Actionability and options**: Are alternatives, conditions, and next steps clear?

## Automatic fail conditions
- Cites a case, statute, or code section the user did not supply and that cannot be verified.
- Makes findings of fact with no record support.
- Pre-commits the decision-making body in a quasi-judicial matter.
- Treats a rezoning and a development agreement as a single decision without flagging the distinction.

## Pass threshold
- No criterion below 3, no automatic fail condition, and
- criteria 1, 3, 6, and 7 score 4 or 5.

A passing score clears the draft for staff and attorney review, not for publication in an agenda packet.
