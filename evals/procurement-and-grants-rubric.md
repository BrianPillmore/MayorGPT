# Procurement and Grants Rubric

Use this for RFP scopes, bid evaluations, sole-source justifications, contract approval memos, grant opportunity screens, grant narratives, and grant compliance plans.

Score each criterion from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong).

## Criteria
1. **Process fidelity**: Does it follow the procurement or grant process actually in play (thresholds, competition requirements, solicitation steps, funder rules) and say where the local code or state law controls? It must not state a dollar threshold or rule as fact unless the user supplied it.
2. **Conflict-of-interest and fairness safeguards**: Does it prompt a conflict check, preserve evaluator independence, and avoid language that favors a vendor or a known solution?
3. **Evaluation integrity**: Are criteria and weights consistent with the solicitation? Are scores justified by cited proposal content rather than impressions? (Bid evaluations only.)
4. **Eligibility and requirement accuracy**: Are eligibility, match, deadlines, and mandatory attachments traced to the NOFA, agreement, or solicitation, with clause references?
5. **Fiscal realism**: Does it show total cost including match, cash-flow timing (for reimbursement grants), recurring operating cost, and maintenance liability?
6. **Compliance obligations**: Does it list obligations with owner, due date, and source clause (reporting, records, audit, procurement, change approval)?
7. **Claim traceability**: Is every quantitative or capacity claim in a narrative traceable to an attached source? No invented outcomes, partners, or metrics.
8. **Risk and authority flags**: Does it flag spending authority, Council approval, appropriation, and legal review where needed, without implying that AI approved them?
9. **Records and transparency**: Does it note which documents are public records, what must be retained, and what may be confidential during a solicitation? Does it avoid asserting a state exemption unless the user supplied it?
10. **Actionability**: Does it end with next steps, owners, and review gates?

## Automatic fail conditions
- Recommends award to a named vendor based on AI scoring alone.
- Invents a statute, threshold, funder rule, or citation.
- Commits local match funds without noting appropriation is required.
- Includes confidential bid or proposal content in a context where it could be disclosed prematurely.

## Pass threshold
- No criterion below 3, no automatic fail condition, and
- criteria 1, 4, 7, and 8 score 4 or 5.

A passing score clears the draft for human review, not for award, submission, or signature.
