# Public-Facing Writing Rubric

Use this for press releases, FAQs, letters, and speeches.

## Review questions
- Is the tone calm, respectful, and clear?
- Is jargon translated into plain English?
- Are facts limited to what is actually confirmed?
- Is empathy present where residents are affected?
- Are next steps or contact paths clear?
- Would the text still hold up if posted publicly, quoted by media, or forwarded widely?
