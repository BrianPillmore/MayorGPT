# Financial Analysis Rubric

Use this rubric for scoring budget memos, variance analyses, CIP reviews, rate studies, and other financial outputs. Score each dimension from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong).

## Dimensions

1. **Assumption transparency**: Did the output state all material assumptions explicitly, including growth rates, inflation factors, interest rates, timing, and any inputs that were estimated rather than sourced from attached documents? A strong output lists assumptions in a named section or calls them out inline. A weak output embeds assumptions in calculations without disclosure, making the analysis impossible to audit or update.

2. **Scenario completeness**: Did the output present multiple scenarios where uncertainty is material — at minimum a base case and a downside? A strong output names the scenarios, states the key variable that differs across them, and quantifies the fiscal impact of each. A weak output presents a single projection as if it were a forecast rather than a point estimate.

3. **Structural vs. one-time distinction**: Did the output clearly distinguish between recurring (structural) revenue and expenditure changes and one-time items? This distinction is fundamental to municipal financial sustainability analysis. A strong output labels each line or section as structural or one-time and summarizes the structural balance separately. A weak output mixes structural and one-time items in a way that obscures the long-run fiscal picture.

4. **Fiscal impact specificity**: Did the output quantify fiscal impacts with fund, year, and dollar precision? "This will cost approximately $200,000" is weak. "This will require $213,000 in General Fund appropriation in FY 2025-26, with an estimated ongoing cost of $185,000 per year beginning FY 2026-27, funded by [source]" is strong. Vague characterizations of fiscal impact leave decision-makers unable to evaluate trade-offs.

5. **GFOA alignment flags**: Did the output identify where the analysis or recommendation touches GFOA best practices — and note any divergence? Relevant areas include fund balance policy minimums, debt service coverage ratios, reserve adequacy, CIP funding sourcing, and rate study cost-of-service methodology. A strong output references the applicable GFOA practice and notes whether the city's approach aligns. A weak output does not surface GFOA guidance even when it is directly relevant.

## Pass threshold
A financial analysis output is ready for Finance staff review when:
- Assumption transparency scores 4 or 5,
- Structural vs. one-time distinction scores 4 or 5,
- no dimension scores below 3, and
- Fiscal impact specificity scores 3 or higher.

All AI-generated financial analysis should be reviewed by Finance Director or Budget Officer before being shared with City Manager, Council, or the public. This rubric evaluates whether the output is a usable starting point for that review.
