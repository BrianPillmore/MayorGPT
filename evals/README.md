# Evals

Use these rubrics to score AI-generated output before it goes anywhere that matters. Apply a rubric before sending output to the HR director, city attorney, Council, or the public. A passing score means the draft is a safe starting point for human review — not that it is ready to use without one.

---

## The 7 rubrics

| Rubric | Scores |
|---|---|
| `municipal-output-rubric.md` | General-purpose: whether any municipal draft stays in its role lane, surfaces process issues, distinguishes facts from assumptions, flags fiscal and legal risk, and ends with actionable next steps. |
| `financial-analysis-rubric.md` | Whether a budget memo, variance analysis, or CIP review states assumptions transparently, presents multiple scenarios, separates structural from one-time items, quantifies fiscal impact precisely, and flags GFOA alignment issues. |
| `legal-analysis-rubric.md` | Whether a legal memo or ordinance draft correctly identifies the legal issue, grounds conclusions in specific authority, calibrates risk accurately, hedges appropriately, and names the review gates required before the city can act. |
| `hr-personnel-rubric.md` | Whether a hiring kit, disciplinary memo, investigation intake, or performance review respects due process, uses only job-related criteria, separates documented facts from conclusions, protects employee privacy, and flags mandatory legal review points. |
| `agenda-packet-rubric.md` | Whether a draft agenda packet includes all intended items, contains action language, identifies missing attachments, notes fiscal impact and legal review status, and makes the requested action obvious to decision-makers. |
| `emergency-brief-rubric.md` | Whether an emergency situation report separates confirmed facts from preliminary reports, states what changed since the last update, shows community impacts and operational actions, identifies resource needs, and aligns public messaging with the operational picture. |
| `public-facing-writing-rubric.md` | Whether a press release, FAQ, letter, or speech is calm and clear, translates jargon, limits facts to what is confirmed, shows empathy where residents are affected, and would hold up if quoted publicly or forwarded widely. |

---

## Pass threshold

Each rubric defines its own pass threshold. In general, passing means no dimension scores below 3 and the highest-stakes dimensions (assumption transparency, due process, issue identification, factual vs. conclusory distinction) score 4 or 5. A passing score clears the draft for the next human review step — it does not clear it for distribution or official use.
